\begin{enumerate}
    \item[1.4] An alternative representation of abts, called \textit{astract binding graphs}, or \textit{abg's} for short, are directed graphs constructed as follows: 
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Free variables are atomic nodes with no outgoing edges
        \item Operators with $n$ arguments are $n$-ary nodes, with one outgoing edge directed at each of their children
        \item Abstractors are nodes with one edge directed to the scope of the abstracted variable
        \item Bound variables are back edges directed at the abstractor that introduced it
    \end{enumerate}
    Give a precise definition of the sort-index family $\cG[\cX]$ of abstract binding graphs
\end{enumerate}
